The app

The free app includes one desk, the presets 25/5, 50/10, and 90/15, one custom interval, three local soundscapes, a today strip, a Lock Screen countdown, a Home Screen widget, and screen dim. Desk Pro adds unlimited desks, the rest of the sound library, audio import, a weekly chart, a desk-day streak, break prompts, Ink and OLED themes, CSV export, and the Start Desk shortcut.

Subscriptions

Desk Pro Monthly is USD $9.99 per month. Desk Pro Yearly is USD $39.99 per year. Those are the intended App Store prices; the paywall shows the price the store returns when it is connected.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID. The plan renews until you cancel in Settings at least 24 hours before the period ends. Renewal is charged within 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Any unused portion of a free trial, if one is offered later, is forfeited when you buy a subscription, where Apple’s rules say so.

Restore purchases is available in the app. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.
