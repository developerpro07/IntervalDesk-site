Privacy

Interval Desk · 26 September 2026

Interval Desk is a focus timer. It does not ask you to create an account. Desks, tasks, and session history stay on your iPhone.

What stays on the device

Interval presets, the desk name, the task label, soundscape choice, and session history are stored locally. Soundscapes that ship with the app are generated on the device. Audio you import is copied into the app’s storage. The Home Screen widget reads a small summary from an App Group on the same device.

Purchases

Desk Pro is sold by Apple. The app asks StoreKit, and RevenueCat when a public SDK key is configured, whether the entitlement intervaldesk_pro is active. We do not receive your card number. Purchase history is used to unlock Desk Pro on this device.

Analytics and crashes

If the build includes a Firebase configuration for the project intervaldesk-ios, the app sends product-interaction events (screens, timer actions, paywall, and purchases) and crash diagnostics to Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. That can include a device identifier used by Firebase. It is not used to track you across other companies’ apps, and it is not linked to your name or email. If that configuration file is absent, those calls are not started.

The app does not use the advertising identifier and does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Notifications

The first time you start a session, iOS may ask for notification permission so the app can tell you when a block ends. If you decline, the timer still runs.

What we do not collect

No photos, contacts, location, health data, or microphone recordings. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Support

This page is also the support URL. Subscription management is in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. For product questions, use the App Store product page until a separate mailbox is published.
